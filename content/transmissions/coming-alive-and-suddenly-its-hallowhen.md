So on we went through 2014 like an express train. At every station more DJs got on, like Porsche Steve, CP30, The Mystery DJ, Charlie n' Cooper, DJ Scott. The return of Nocturnatina and Becca's Kids Show. Things are good and they're only going to get better.

I write this as we return to streaming. The timing couldn't be better. Already the response is exceptional, and on a Saturday night with the Gumbo Show to share with the world. Ah yes, we're finally starting to rock again. Check out our schedule here on the website.

Meanwhile the old soundboard is starting to give up. It's channel roulette. The electric bill needs payin'. Chip (our computer) needs better memory. Better sealant for the roof would be nice too. Sounds like a Santa list. Well, it is — and you folks are going to be our Santa by coming to the Hallowhen? Funraiser and supporting this ongoing experiment in "Pure Community Radio."

Like always, we won't hold the airwaves hostage to raise money. Instead we like to throw a party with great music, food and drinks and hopefully raise some funds in the process. We sincerely request your presence.
