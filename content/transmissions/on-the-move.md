  to the old Catholic Church — with big plans for the Ward community.

We are going to be moving next to the old Catholic Church by October. It's the deal we made with the Town. They were gracious enough to let us be in front of the Town Hall to get started, but this location has more parking, its own electricity, and we have some great future plans that will further benefit the Ward community.

Thanks to the amazing turnout for the "Revival" on July 1st, we raised enough money to pay for the necessary upgrades to our equipment. THANK YOU WARD! And thanks to all the people and businesses that contributed to this extremely successful event.

One of the things we're looking forward to is our Way High Radio Presents series, which will start later in 2012. These will be intimate events that will feature a wide variety of local talent. Another fun event will be our Acoustic Pancake Breakfasts — start your day with good food n' great music. Lots of cool ideas, and we want to know yours too. If you're local, become a DJ or volunteer your talents in some other way. It's easy. Contact us.
