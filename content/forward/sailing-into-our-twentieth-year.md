Okay, I know. It's been a really long time since we posted anything to this blog. Where to begin.

Our scheduled shows continue to showcase the diversity in our mountain habitat. We've had some great DJs come and go but the crew continues to grow with a desire to share. Working with the Colorado Community Radio Network has really helped enlarge our listening audience. It also creates a larger sense of community. Our shows are shared on 93.1 KNED, 95.5 Boulder Free Radio and 93.1 Greenlight Radio in the Boulder Valley.

We have also had two successful years of broadcasting from ground zero at Frozen Dead Guy Days with DJs from all member stations keeping the vibe going all weekend long. Our Tech / DJ Sky Fly has been hard at work constantly upgrading the automation — station IDs, PSAs, scripts to auto-reboot Chip (our computer) when the power goes down, and a remote broadcasting platform so we can broadcast from events like Boulder's Big Hootenany.

## The Cold Spring Fire

Another landmark event this summer was our response to the Cold Spring Fire in Nederland. On July 9th we were alerted about a fire; as we prepared to broadcast, the power went out. Thanks to protocols built over the last few years, we powered the studio with a generator and Nedernet's internet with a car and a power inverter. Within a half hour we were back broadcasting and relaying on KNED.

For the next five days a dedicated DJ crew worked 24/7 to update people with any info available through Boulder OEM. This fulfills an aspect of our charter: to serve the mountain community in times of disaster.
